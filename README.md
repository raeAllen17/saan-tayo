# Saan Tayo?

A browsable catalog of real restaurant menus, starting with one small city.

## Phase 1: Menu data

The first phase is simple: get restaurant menus (categories, items, descriptions, prices) onto the website. The restaurant or our team can enter the data.

The menu content is the core asset, because a browsable catalog of real menus is what makes the platform useful to diners.

## Why start in one small city

A small city lets us onboard most of the local restaurants by hand and build a catalog that feels complete. Diners will use it only if it covers the places they'd actually consider, so local density matters more than breadth.

Many small-city restaurants also have no good web presence for their menu, so the listing is an easy sell to them.

## Goals

- Cover most local restaurants in a single small city.
- Let restaurants or our team enter menu data: categories, items, descriptions, and prices.
- Make the catalog feel complete enough that diners rely on it.
